# Dates, repetition and reminders / 日期、重复与提醒

- The effective application time zone follows the OS unless overridden in Settings. A date-only task stores a calendar day with no instant or zone. A timed task stores a UTC instant and its IANA display zone. Changing the application zone changes which calendar day an instant belongs to in smart views; it does not rewrite the stored instant or date-only value.
- `Today` includes unfinished overdue tasks, tasks due today and tasks planned today. `Planned` uses a selected natural month, with shared list/month presentations. It includes tasks whose planned day or displayed deadline is inside that month, including past dates; a task may also appear in Today. `Anytime` contains unfinished tasks with neither a due date nor a planned day. Completed and deleted tasks are excluded.
- 手用输入支持「今天」「明天」「后天」「下周五」「本月底」、中文时刻（如「下午三点半」）和 ISO 日期。时间不明确时，「早上」默认 08:00、「上午」09:00、「中午」12:00、「下午」15:00、「傍晚」18:00、「晚上」20:00、「凌晨」01:00；创建前可以在详情里调整。「下周」按周一开始；单写「周五」取今天或之后最近的周五。
- Recurrence uses the `rrule` library with a restricted daily, weekly (including multiple weekdays/workdays) and monthly (one date or one numbered weekday) subset. Interval is one. A rule may have **either** an inclusive local `until` day **or** a count from its original start. Excluded dates still consume their original position in a count-limited series.
- **Monthly day 31 clamps to the last day of a short month**, e.g. January 31 → February 28 (29 in a leap year) → March 31 → April 30. Monthly days 29 and 30 clamp similarly. A numbered weekday that does not exist in a month is omitted according to RRULE (e.g. a fifth Monday).
- Instances are generated from original planned dates, not completion dates, with a unique `(series_id, occurrence_key)` key. Repeated generation does not reset completion, skip or rescheduling. Marking an instance done, skipped or moved affects that instance only. Legacy instance rescheduling preserves the series wall clock. Calendar rescheduling preserves the displayed wall clock and stores an explicit single-instance instant; it does not change the original series key or later instances.
- Timed repetitions retain their local clock time across daylight-saving changes. A nonexistent local time in a spring-forward gap is shifted forward by the time-zone library; its identity retains the original planned clock time. Ambiguous fall-back times use Luxon's time-zone resolution. Date-only repeating reminders retain their local reminder time across DST. A timed repetition's reminder offset is an elapsed duration relative to the instance deadline.
- The app generates a rolling 90-day future window plus instances from the series start; when that window has no future occurrence, it includes the first valid later instance, with a 10,000-instance safety limit. Completed/skipped instances remain persisted. Remove a recurrence before replacing its rule; removing a recurrence removes its generated instance history and returns the task to an ordinary task.
- Calendar month browsing materializes only the requested bounded range (at most 62 days per request), merges persisted moved-in instances, and preserves COUNT, exclusions and the monthly clamp. It does not materialize every intervening day when jumping to a distant month. Newly materialized past reminders are recorded as suppressed, so browsing history does not trigger a notification flood.
- Calendar moves of an existing timed deadline require explicit resolution of nonexistent or ambiguous displayed wall times. Moving only the planned day leaves deadline, reminder and notification history unchanged. Moving a deadline with a reminder requires a keep/follow choice; timed reminders follow by elapsed offset and date-only reminders by displayed local day/time. A reminder that itself falls into an unresolved DST gap/overlap must be kept or edited explicitly first. Explicit due moves and undo record newly past reminders without immediate replay.
- There is one reminder per task/instance. An atomic notification log prevents the same target and scheduled reminder instant from firing repeatedly. On startup, overdue reminders are consumed once and the app shows one summary. No reminder delivery is promised after **Quit**, while the computer is off/asleep, or when the OS suppresses notifications. Keeping the application running in the tray allows periodic checking (every 15 seconds).

Run `npm test -- tests/schedule.test.ts` for parsing, smart-view, timezone, monthly-clamp, instance-idempotency, DST and reminder-deduplication acceptance tests.
